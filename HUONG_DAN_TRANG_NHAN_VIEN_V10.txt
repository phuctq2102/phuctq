V10 - TRANG NHAN VIEN THEO MAU TIM KHU VUC

Da them src/EmployeeCheck.jsx va CSS; chi thay giao dien khi tai khoan NHAN_VIEN vao tab Phan cong.
Khong thay doi Admin, Supabase Edge Functions, bang SQL, hay CT_KV goc.

Hien thi: ma SP, ten SP, SL dem, SL so, GT chenh lech, trang thai; chi tiet DSCL; CT_KV/BC; khu vuc, barcode, DVT nhap, SL nhap, SL thuc te; them KV; cap nhat trang thai.

CAN KIEM TRA TREN SUPABASE TRUOC KHI SU DUNG THAT:
1. Nhan vien can quyen SELECT DSCL, CT_KV, BC, DIEU_CHINH theo ma duoc phan cong.
2. Nhan vien can quyen INSERT/UPDATE DIEU_CHINH va UPDATE PHAN_CONG cho chinh ma duoc phan cong. ZIP khong tu dong mo RLS vi co nguy co lo du lieu.
3. Khong co transaction nguyen tu cho cap nhat nhieu dong; neu loi giua chung, can kiem tra truoc khi bam lai.
4. Dong BC khong co CT_KV ID chua duoc ho tro chinh sua truc tiep; co thong bao thay vi luu sai.
5. Khong co kha nang kiem thu live tren du an Supabase cua ban tu moi truong nay.
6. Phan hien thi chenh lech dua tren DSCL va DIEU_CHINH; chua dong bo FINAL tu dong.

Cai dat: giai nen, sao chep .env.local cua ban vao thu muc goc, npm install, npm run dev.
