V10 - CHUYEN CO CHE DONG BO FINAL TU GAS SANG SUPABASE

1. SAO LUU bang final_lines va dieu_chinh trong Supabase truoc khi chay SQL.
2. Mo Supabase SQL Editor bang tai khoan quan tri, chay SUPABASE_FINAL_AUTO.sql mot lan.
3. Xem bang ket qua cuoi: 10005170 delta +1; 10005201 delta +2; 10141959 delta +2 (neu du lieu khong thay doi).
4. Kiem tra tab Final va tai lai trang. Neu giao dien van hien 0, kiem tra RLS SELECT tren dieu_chinh, dscl, ct_kv, final_lines.
5. Sau moi INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE dieu_chinh, trigger dong bo final_lines trong cung transaction. Neu ghi final_lines that bai, thao tac ghi dieu_chinh se bao loi (khong am tham bo qua).
6. SQL khong xoa CT_KV, DSCL hay BC. Barcode trong DS ma hang tiep tuc an theo ban React truoc.
7. SQL luu 1 dong tong hop cho moi ma SP co thay doi, KHONG phai chi tiet tung KV. Phan QR/Excel cua giao dien React van dung logic hien tai.
8. Quan trong: nhung tai khoan NV khong co quyen INSERT/UPDATE dieu_chinh se van can chinh RLS rieng. Khong tat RLS.
9. Neu co nhieu session sua cung mot ma dong thoi, can kiem thu concurrency tren moi truong test truoc khi dua vao production.
